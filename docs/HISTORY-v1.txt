WHAT IS NEW
1. Full-screen shooting with visible shutter and safe-area layout. Options opens
   a bottom sheet; Compose and cm / m are available from the shooting toolbar.
2. Compose compares background detail and brightness, suggests a quieter side
   or a tighter portrait, and checks for a possible horizon. Use this framing
   applies the suggestion to the actual guide and movement calculations.
3. Best of 3 saves three real frames and highlights a candidate using visible
   eye state, sharpness and frame change. All three remain available to select.
   Auto capture with Best of 3 waits for alignment, fresh open-eye detection,
   low frame movement and sufficient estimated sharpness for two seconds.
4. Travel, Café, Full body and Seated packs, with seven poses and hand/gaze tips.
   Seated guides use knees as the framing boundary.

DISTANCES IN CM / M
Tap cm / m, enter the person's height in centimeters and current camera-to-person
distance in meters. Calibrate upright with the full person visible and phone level.
The guide estimates lateral and forward/back movement from image geometry.
Keep the person, posture, lens and zoom unchanged; recalibrate after changes.
These are estimates based on entered values, not depth-sensor measurements.
Seated poses use visual guidance: standing-height calibration does not apply.

OTHER FEATURES
Live single-person pose tracking and current/target skeletons; 3:4 and 1:1 crop;
front/rear camera switch; grid and guide toggles; 3/5 second timer; English voice;
photo import; local gallery; JPG download and supported-device share sheet.
The interface is English, royal blue #3157ED, white #FFFFFF and black #111217.

PRIVACY AND LIMITS
Camera frames, pose/face inference and saved photos stay on the device.
No image uploads, API key, analytics or external inference service is used.
Model files are hosted with the app. Photos are stored in browser IndexedDB;
download important photos because browser storage can be cleared or unavailable.
The camera stops when the page is hidden. Other app views pause its video track.
Composition is based on pixel brightness and background edges, not semantic
scene recognition. A wall or roof edge can be mistaken for a possible horizon.
Eye checks need a clear face. Unknown results block quality-based auto capture;
the manual shutter remains available. Disable Best of 3 for pose-only auto.
Sharpness and motion scores are estimates, not camera sensor measurements.
Gaze tips are instructions; gaze direction is not detected.
Guide scores measure target alignment, not beauty or photographic quality.
One person is supported. Occlusion, backlight and movement reduce reliability.
Native LiDAR/ARKit depth, group composition, ProRAW and native high-resolution
still capture are not part of this web release. Video resolution is browser-limited.
The new features have been tested in a responsive browser with synthetic video,
not on physical iPhone hardware. The user previously confirmed camera startup
worked in iPhone Safari; performance and sharing need device testing.

RUN LOCALLY
Mac: double-click Start.command (Python 3 required).
Other platforms: python3 serve.py
Do not open index.html as a local file. Camera access requires HTTPS except
localhost; an iPhone cannot use the Mac's 127.0.0.1 address.
For permanent hosting upload the contents of frame-deploy.zip to static HTTPS
hosting. No build step or secret is required. No Sites deployment is used.
